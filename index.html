10C Assignment 1: Large Format Print

The heartbeat creates sound waves. The pattern of the heartbeat, if healthy, can be considered mathematically a fractal. The illustration of the wave forms of the harmonic series also kind of reminds me of a fractal (I dont think it mathematically is but it reminded me of one). Fractals are proven to be soothing to the human body, our nervous system naturally calms down when gazing upon images of them. There is a proposition in the music theory book about this particular healing effect in the form of sound waves of the harmonic series. The proper harmony alignment promotes harmony in the human body. To some, there is an innate human proclivity to be attracted to these patterns, be it visually or through sound-perhaps the fractality of our heartbeat gives us an innate drive to find this or create this kind of pattern in our reality. I propose that patterns and fractals of sound and vision are humans’ instinctive way of expressing the need to find homeostasis within the body and within consciousness. Like an instinctive search for balance, which is why we feel soothed when looking at/hearing these things.

I’m trying to visually establish a relationship between humankind and these concepts using the images presented in these textbooks. Perhaps in conjunction with blurbs of text hinting at this relationship.
